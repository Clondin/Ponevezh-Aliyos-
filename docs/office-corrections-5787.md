# Israeli office catalog corrections — 5787

Approved on 25 August 2026. These instructions supersede the original frozen
5787 inventory where they conflict.

- Sukkos is not offered.
- Hoshana Rabbah remains in the Ponevez Yeshiva minyan and includes Pesichas
  HaAron for Hoshanos.
- Simchas Torah night has three aliyos in the Nusach Ashkenaz minyanim. In the
  current six-minyan model this means every minyan except Chayei Avraham — Edot
  HaMizrach.
- Rosh Hashanah I and II each use two Sifrei Torah, with separate Hotza'ah
  VeHachnasah and Hagbah VeGelilah sponsorships for each Sefer.
- When Rosh Hashanah I is Shabbos, Mincha includes Hotza'ah VeHachnasah, three
  aliyos, and Hagbah VeGelilah. The calendar generator applies this only in
  years where the first day is Shabbos.
- Yom Kippur Shacharis uses two Sifrei Torah, with separate Hotza'ah
  VeHachnasah and Hagbah VeGelilah sponsorships for each Sefer.
- Ne'ilah has one Pesichas HaAron per minyan.
- Minyan labels are Grodna Yeshiva, Perlman Avreichim Minyan, Yeshiva Ketana,
  and Chayei Avraham — Edot HaMizrach.

Purchasing an aliyah is a sponsorship, not a guarantee that the purchaser will
personally receive it. The Yeshiva assigns aliyos to Roshei Yeshiva, Rabbanim,
and congregants at its discretion. The guaranteed benefit is that a Mi
Shebeirach will be recited for the person or persons chosen by the purchaser.
